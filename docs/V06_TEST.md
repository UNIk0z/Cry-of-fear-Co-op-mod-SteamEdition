# v0.6 test run

Both players: close the game, run "Install.cmd" from the v0.6 folder, then
"Run Co-op Mod.cmd". The host sends the newest
cryoffear\storycoop_logs\rc4-*.log after the session.

1. **Loading.** Change maps: the first player in waits ("Waiting for players
   1/2") until the other has loaded.
2. **Falls.** Fall into a pit: back beside the partner after about 2 s, with
   the same inventory.
3. **College emergency exit** (s4_college1dark) and **the ladder hole after
   the floor breaks** (s4_down -> apartment): you arrive where you left, not at
   the map's start.
4. **Harbor door** (s4_hamn, the Sawrunner chase): when the door locks behind
   one player, the other appears beside them.
5. **Ladders.** One climbs, the other presses E on the same ladder: the second
   is moved to the top/bottom when the first gets off.
6. **Doctor's door and the warehouse area:** partners stay visible. If not,
   send the log (VISIBLE lines).
7. **Subway (Chapter 5):** no Chapter 4 fuses in the inventory; the subway
   fuses can be taken and placed.
