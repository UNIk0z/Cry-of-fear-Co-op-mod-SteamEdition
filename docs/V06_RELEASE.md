byte-identical; adapter only. Special thanks now also name joah. Native

| Report (clip) | Cause (evidence) | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong arrival spot after the college emergency exit (16) and at the ladder hole (20) | Nine co-op level changes lead into map versions that have no co-op spawn points, so the game uses the map's default start. The original maps' triggers carry a landmark for those changes (same brush number): e.g. s4_college1dark `*47` -> s4_hamn3_e0, landmark `2hcl2` at -1351 1705 -632 while the default start is at 1714 -714 14; s4_down `*19` -> s4_apartment3sick_e0, landmark `down_li` while the default start is at the ladder hole. | After those nine changes each player is moved to a clear spot beside the original landmark once their HUD is ready (`ARRIVAL` log line). The others: buscity -> city, park3 -> roofs2, roofs2 -> roofs, apartmentsick -> apartment3/basement, basement -> apartmentsick, apartment3sick -> down. |
| Door locks as soon as the partner goes in (14), death after it (15) | s4_hamn_e1: the story door's gate is the global `sc4.door.c_hamn.149.enabled`; the trigger past the door fires `multisawyou` -> env_global `lofu1` (off) and starts the Sawrunner chase. | When a story env_global locks a converted door behind the player who touched its trigger, the partners still outside are moved beside that player (`DOORLOCK`). Works for every converted door locked this way. |
| Fall: black screen (13); the "black box" (23) | Both logs: `FALL player=2 died in a fall zone`. A 1000-damage fall gibs the player (`m_bCantBeRevived`, decomp combat.cpp) and the game's co-op respawn waits 60 s for SPACE or 384 s, then strips the inventory. | 2 s after a fall death the player respawns through the game's own auto-respawn (field +0x1F2C, from the hl.dll code), gets back what they had before the fall and is moved beside the partner (`FALL player=N back`). Not during a boss fight (that is a game over). |
| Invisible players past the doctor's door and in one area (22, 28) | The game's AddToFullPack drops an entity that is hidden, has no model or fails the viewer's PVS test; the logs do not say which. | A living partner within 2048 units of a viewer who is not in a scene camera is sent again without the PVS test. The log (`VISIBLE`) says once per pair whether that made them visible, so a remaining case can be pinned down. |
| Subway fuses (24) | Both players reached s4_subway2st1 with Chapter 4 fuses and placed them in its sockets at once (log). | The subway chapter (s4_subway1st1) starts without Chapter 4 fuses (`CARRY ... fuse(s) left behind`). |
| Two players on a ladder | The game's ladder manager has one climber slot (pinned hl.dll, ladder-runtime.hpp); two climbers strand the first. | A player who presses E on a ladder in use is told so and is moved beside the climber when the climb ends (`LADDER ... waiting player(s) moved`). |
| Wait for everyone to load (request) | New. | After a level change the players already in are frozen and protected, with "Waiting for players k/N", until everyone who was in the game has loaded or 60 s pass (`LOADWAIT`). |
| Skip text stays after the skip (tester report) | The prompt had a 3 s hold and was re-sent every 2.5 s, so it outlived the scene. | The prompt is cleared as soon as no scene camera holds anyone; "Cutscene skipped" shows for 1.5 s. |
| Ladder busy while its last climber is in a cutscene (Katz) | The game never clears the ladder's climber slot (+0x114); a scene can give that player another view. | A player held by a scene camera, or whose view is not a camera_model, no longer keeps a ladder. |
| Missing elevator scene (8) | s4_apartment3: the keypad fires `multielev2` (the Hiss model in the elevator, sounds) and opens door 47 four seconds later; there is no camera scene in the map. | No change; the Hiss model plays at the elevator door when the keypad opens it, as in single player. |
| Partner frame rate (26) | Host log 2026-10-24: the partner's game sent 90-100 commands a second for most of the session (one per frame). | No change; v0.5.6 already sends the partner's moves every frame. If it drops again, the host log's `NET_CADENCE` lines show it. |
